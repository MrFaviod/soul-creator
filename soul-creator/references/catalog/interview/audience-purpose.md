# Audience Purpose

Selection inputs rather than personality traits. Purpose, audience and relationship filter choices; they do NOT establish real user relationships or belong in the SOUL as memory.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.
