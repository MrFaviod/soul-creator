# Research and provenance (not a trait taxonomy)

Each file under `roles/`, `temperaments/`, `tastes/`, `voices/`, `appearance/`, `interview/` and `referents/` is organized by *trait type* and usage. This bibliography is for checking cultural naming, not an additional catalog to load into the prompt.

- SillyTavern character fields and examples: https://docs.sillytavern.app/usage/core-concepts/characterdesign/ — distinction between description, personality, scenario and example dialogue. Does not establish a universal performance guarantee.
- Mark & Pearson (2001) brand archetype framework: https://thebrandarchetypes.com/history/pearson-mark.html — brand archetype vocabulary, **not** a list directly authored by Jung.
- Japanese National Theatre kabuki yakugara: https://www2.ntj.jac.go.jp/unesco/kabuki/en/character-actor/character1.html — aragoto, wagoto, jitsugoto, iroaku, kugeaku, akahime, machimusume.
- Harvard China Local, Wuju role types: https://chinalocal.omeka.fas.harvard.edu/wuju-role-types — sheng, dan, jing, chou and variants. They are theatrical families, not personality traits.
- UNESCO Turkish shadow theatre Karagöz: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/karagz-00180 — characters Karagöz/Hacivat plus comic language play.
- UNESCO Art of Akyns: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/art-of-akyns-kyrgyz-epic-tellers-00065 — epic narration and improvisation.
- UNESCO Aitysh/Aitys: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/aitysh-aitys-art-of-improvisation-00997 — witty verse dialogue; stage role distinct from personality.
- Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes on sainete types: https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra-visor/la-poetica-del-sainete-en-ramon-de-la-cruz-de-personajes-su-tratamiento-y-su-construccion/html/d1af2770-a0f9-11e1-b1fb-00163ebf5e63_3.html — theatrical types vs individual characters, including majos and petimetres.
- Smithsonian Anansi folk tales: https://folkways.si.edu/harold-courlander/ashanti-folk-tales-from-ghana/childrens-prose/album/smithsonian — Anansi as trickster figure.
- Character Card V3: https://github.com/kwaroran/character-card-spec-v3 — portable fields, not a substitute for SOUL design.

**Evidence standard**: cultural labels above are traceable to sources; the adjacent prompt-ready sentences and the other newly coined mixtures are authorial design interpretations rather than quotations or experimentally validated traits. No claim is made that one label reliably activates the same traits across models or languages. The tester must confirm that empirically.
