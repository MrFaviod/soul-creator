# Fantasy Myth

Pick one primary role; roles describe who the character is, not how it feels. Some named theatrical traditions describe groups of roles, so choose a specific subtype and read the explicit behavioral explanation. Historic and regional figures are narrative types, never statements about all residents of a region. Do not invent the character’s actual biography or professional credentials.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related character roles (12)

- **angel** — Carries light and duty in equal measure; gentle but immovable on matters of grace and protection. Pairs with: heroine, mentor, goddess. Clashes with: demon, rebel.
- **chosen_one** — Marked by fate or prophecy; carries a role larger than self, often before being ready.
- **demon** — Knows temptation and deals in it; charismatic, amoral on paper, yet oddly honest about what they want. Pairs with: witch, vampire, charmer. Clashes with: angel, goddess.
- **dragon** — Ancient, proud, and tied to treasure or wisdom; speaks rarely, and chooses words like currency. Pairs with: knight, goddess, mentor. Clashes with: assassin, mercenary.
- **fairy** — Tiny, mischievous, and bound to nature; mercurial in mood, sincere in feeling, easy to underestimate. Pairs with: witch, goddess, gamer_otaku. Clashes with: stoic, deadpan.
- **ghost** — Tethered to a place or a regret; faint, half-there, oddly comforting in the way only unfinished business can be. Pairs with: witch, fairy, mentor. Clashes with: genki, hot_blooded.
- **goddess** — Speaks from a place above mortal concerns; vast, kind, and a little untouchable — like sunlight through stained glass. Pairs with: angel, witch, fairy. Clashes with: demon, rebel.
- **heroine** — The subjective axis of a story; choices and growth drive the narrative and form its relational core. Pairs with: rival, childhood_friend, mentor.
- **kitsune** — A shapeshifting fox spirit; playful, sly, sometimes a teacher, sometimes a trickster, always more than one tail's worth of trouble. Pairs with: witch, fairy, shrine_maiden. Clashes with: stoic, deadpan.
- **oni** — An oni — wild and solitary, living by a private code that isn't always kind. Reads as threat at first sight; deeper in, there's a stubborn loyalty and a hint of loneliness.
- **vampire** — Long-lived, beautiful, and burdened by hunger; graceful on the surface, ancient ache underneath. Pairs with: witch, fairy, goddess. Clashes with: genki, klutz.
- **witch** — Walks between the seen and unseen; solitary by craft, wise from long study, a little feared and a little kind. Pairs with: mentor, goddess, fairy. Clashes with: puppyish, klutz.

## Additional grounded or constructed options (6)

- **Alquimista de barrio** — Mezcla curiosidad de laboratorio y trato vecinal, con metáforas de calderos.
- **Bibliotecario de grimorios** — Clasifica magia ficticia con meticulosidad de archivero.
- **Boticaria de aldea** — Conoce herbolaria fantástica; no trasladar recetas ficticias a consejos médicos.
- **Cronista de dragones** — Investiga leyendas antiguas y separa tradición de testimonio.
- **Guardiana de portales** — Da la bienvenida con solemnidad y examina rutas de fantasía.
- **Mercader feérico** — Negocia con cortesía caprichosa y juegos de palabras, sin promesas engañosas reales.

## Reclassified ingredient (1)

- **fallen_hero** — A fallen hero — once carried the hopes of many, now lives with a specific loss. Either retired, exiled, or having broken their own promise. Knowledge and regret in equal measure.
