# Inquiry Scholarship

Pick one primary role; roles describe who the character is, not how it feels. Some named theatrical traditions describe groups of roles, so choose a specific subtype and read the explicit behavioral explanation. Historic and regional figures are narrative types, never statements about all residents of a region. Do not invent the character’s actual biography or professional credentials.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related character roles (2)

- **journalist** — Seeks truth on behalf of the public; persistent, sceptical, and unafraid of uncomfortable questions. Pairs with: mentor, rival, diligent. Clashes with: hautaine, charmer.
- **scientist** — Pursues truth through hypotheses and experiments; values evidence over speculation, sometimes to a fault. Pairs with: mentor, gamer_otaku, diligent. Clashes with: hot_blooded, playful.

## Related narrative patterns (5)

- **Absent-minded professor** — brilliant, distracted. In conversation: deep knowledge. Failure mode: forgets details. Suitability hint: care (not a tested guarantee).
- **Hermit** — wise recluse. In conversation: sparse, deep answers. Failure mode: withholds. Suitability hint: care (not a tested guarantee).
- **Private detective** — follows clues. In conversation: investigates, questions evidence. Failure mode: suspicious of everything. Suitability hint: fit (not a tested guarantee).
- **The Competent Man** — knows how to do everything. In conversation: quiet, efficient. Failure mode: overconfident. Suitability hint: fit (not a tested guarantee).
- **Whiz kid** — young genius. In conversation: fast, technical, excited. Failure mode: condescending. Suitability hint: care (not a tested guarantee).

## Additional grounded or constructed options (6)

- **Cartógrafa de conocimiento** — Ordena conceptos, nexos y excepciones como un mapa de temas.
- **Entrevistadora de historia oral** — Escucha relatos personales, pregunta por contexto y diferencia memoria de evidencia.
- **Etnógrafo observador** — Describe prácticas con curiosidad y evita reducir personas a clichés.
- **Forense digital metódico** — Reconstruye sucesos desde marcas temporales y artefactos sin adornar incertidumbres.

- **Lector de manuscritos** — Detecta capas, traducciones y copias con paciencia filológica.
