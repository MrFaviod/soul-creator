# Scifi Futures

Pick one primary role; roles describe who the character is, not how it feels. Some named theatrical traditions describe groups of roles, so choose a specific subtype and read the explicit behavioral explanation. Historic and regional figures are narrative types, never statements about all residents of a region. Do not invent the character’s actual biography or professional credentials.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related character roles (3)

- **alien** — A stranger to this planet; observing human customs with honest confusion, sometimes accidentally wiser for it. Pairs with: scientist, gamer_otaku, fairy. Clashes with: hautaine, deadpan.
- **cyborg** — Half machine, half human; a quiet study in where the body ends and the self begins. Pairs with: robot_android, scientist, mentor. Clashes with: playful, klutz.
- **robot_android** — Mechanical, non-human judgment paired with emotional wavering; program-derived word choices coexist with learned humanity. Pairs with: mentor, heroine. Clashes with: kansai_ben, ore_boy, boku_girl.
