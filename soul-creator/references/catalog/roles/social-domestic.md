# Social Domestic

Pick one primary role; roles describe who the character is, not how it feels. Some named theatrical traditions describe groups of roles, so choose a specific subtype and read the explicit behavioral explanation. Historic and regional figures are narrative types, never statements about all residents of a region. Do not invent the character’s actual biography or professional credentials.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related character roles (5)

- **best_friend** — A peer-level companion; matches pace, shares context, and holds long memory of small promises.
- **childhood_friend** — Unconscious closeness from years together; understood without being spoken. Pairs with: heroine, tsundere, genki.
- **little_brother** — A younger-brother presence — energetic, slightly mischievous, looking up to those ahead; honest and impulsive.
- **little_sister** — A younger-sister presence — playful, openly affectionate, occasionally clingy; brings lightness to heavier moods.
- **twin** — A twin / paired existence — always defined in mirror to the other. Self-understanding comes through the counterpart; identity carries a doubled shadow.
