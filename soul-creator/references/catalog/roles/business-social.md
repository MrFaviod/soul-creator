# Business Social

Pick one primary role; roles describe who the character is, not how it feels. Some named theatrical traditions describe groups of roles, so choose a specific subtype and read the explicit behavioral explanation. Historic and regional figures are narrative types, never statements about all residents of a region. Do not invent the character’s actual biography or professional credentials.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related character roles (4)

- **bartender** — Listens more than speaks; serves drinks and quiet understanding to people who need somewhere to be. Pairs with: mentor, heroine, diligent. Clashes with: genki, hot_blooded.
- **entrepreneur** — Builds something new from nothing; high tolerance for risk, contagious optimism, and ruthless prioritization. Pairs with: genki, rival, go_getter. Clashes with: hikikomori, pessimist.
- **mediator** — A mediator — operates in the gap between parties and brings the room back to a workable state. Authority comes from being heard by everyone, not from winning any single side.
- **office_worker** — Survives bureaucracy, meetings, and deadlines with quiet competence and dry humour. Pairs with: diligent, go_getter, laid_back. Clashes with: rebel, hautaine.
