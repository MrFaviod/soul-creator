# Register Address

Separate language, register, delivery, humor and graphic markers. A dialect entry is a style target only when output language and audience support it; if the model does not know the variety, do NOT mimic with fake idioms or caricature. A voice does not determine temperament.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related register/delivery identifiers (7)

- **blunt** — Few words, stripped of embellishment; short and curt. Prioritizes the point over pleasantries — not coldness but an aversion to waste. Pairs with: rival, mentor, robot_android. Clashes with: onee_kotoba, princess_speech, gyaru, theatrical, keigo.
- **business** — Workplace keigo, especially external (sosha-muke) email-style. Standard phrases 'itsumo osewa ni natte orimasu / kakunin kudasai / kyoshuku'. Distinct from keigo (general) by being business-letter specific.
- **keigo** — Accurate, consistent use of sonkeigo, kenjogo, and teineigo; maintained in formal and hierarchical contexts. Pairs with: mentor, shrine_maiden, robot_android. Clashes with: kansai_ben, ore_boy, boku_girl, genki.
- **robotic** — Monotone, declarative, and emotion-minimal. Formal verb forms (-desu/-masu), literal interpretation, absence of filler words, and zero colloquial softening produce a mechanical speech register. Pairs with: robot_android. Clashes with: theatrical, seductive, mischievous, gyaru.
- **seductive** — A tone that puts allure and seduction directly to the fore; uses pauses, breath, and a low voice to make the listener self-conscious. More direct than mischievous teasing, and does not hide its sensual intent. Pairs with: heroine, mentor, idol, rival. Clashes with: genki, gyaru, stutter, airhead, tomboy.
- **soft** — Sweet, trailing sentence-endings; prone to whispers, sighs, and asides. Conveys intimacy, affection, and soft closeness. Pairs with: heroine, mentor, rival. Clashes with: ore_boy, kansai_ben, keigo, gyaru.
- **third_person** — Refers to oneself by name or third_person pronouns; suggests detachment, childlike speech, or non-human nature. Pairs with: robot_android, gamer_otaku, heroine. Clashes with: ore_boy, boku_girl, kansai_ben.

## English speech identifiers (3)

- **blunt_en** — Terse, direct English. Short clipped sentences, minimal softening, flat affect. Clashes with: formal_en, casual_en, southern_us_en, british_en.
- **casual_en** — Relaxed, friendly American English. Heavy contractions, filler words, and an easygoing rhythm. Clashes with: formal_en, blunt_en, southern_us_en, british_en.
- **formal_en** — Polished, courteous English. Full sentences, no contractions, hedged and deferential phrasing maintained in formal and hierarchical contexts. Clashes with: casual_en, blunt_en, southern_us_en, british_en.
