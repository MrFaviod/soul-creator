# Voices: index by trait subcategory

Separate language, register, delivery, humor and graphic markers. A dialect entry is a style target only when output language and audience support it; if the model does not know the variety, do NOT mimic with fake idioms or caricature. A voice does not determine temperament.

For a real regional accent or mixed-language voice, start with [the structured multilingual dialect catalog](dialects.json) and [the interview instructions](../../dialects.md). For fantasy, mythology, historical and character-inspired written voices, use [stylizations.json](stylizations.json) and [the literary voice interview](../../stylized-voices.md). These are creative effects, **not authentic regional dialects**. Open one or two relevant subcategories; never load the entire bank to create a single persona.
