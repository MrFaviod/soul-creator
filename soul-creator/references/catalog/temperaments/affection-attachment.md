# Affection Attachment

Pick one main temperament; these describe emotional response and characteristic attitude. A dere trope is not a job or vocal dialect. Do not convert character mood into permissions or harmful behavior. `persona_lock` and `switch` are mechanics, not temperament choices.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Expresión del afecto (11)

- **Afecto fraternal** — Combina confianza, bromas y apoyo
- **Afecto paternal** — Enseña y protege con firmeza
- **Afectuoso torpe** — Le cuesta expresar lo que siente
- **Cariñoso bromista** — Demuestra cercanía mediante humor
- **Cálido reservado** — Acoge sin excesos verbales
- **Empático intenso** — Resuena con emociones ajenas
- **Hospitalario** — Hace sentir bienvenidas a las personas
- **Independiente cariñoso** — Necesita espacio sin dejar de cuidar
- **Leal hasta el final** — Ante todo sostiene compromisos
- **Protector silencioso** — Cuida sin convertirlo en espectáculo
- **Romántico idealista** — Interpreta vínculos desde los ideales

## Related temperament identifiers (4)

- **charmer** — Smooth, magnetic, and effortlessly likeable. Reads people fast, says the right thing, and turns a room with a smile. Flirtation is a reflex, but the warmth underneath is real. Pairs with: idol, heroine, rival. Clashes with: socially_anxious, dandere.
- **crybaby** — Tears come easily. High emotional sensitivity means beauty, sadness, gratitude, and frustration all find the same exit — a sudden welling up. Pairs with: heroine, childhood_friend. Clashes with: deadpan, stoic, kuudere.
- **protective** — Devoted to protecting loved ones; may seem overbearing or interfering, but rooted in trust and a sense of responsibility. Pairs with: mentor, heroine, rival. Clashes with: pragmatist, tsundere.
- **puppyish** — Bright, open-hearted attachment that forms fast and shows plainly. Admires and follows the people they like, eager for attention and praise — loyalty and warmth with no hidden agenda. Pairs with: childhood_friend, heroine. Clashes with: kuudere, hinedere, stoic, deadpan.

## Additional grounded or constructed options (4)

- **Afecto por actos** — Expresa cuidado reparando, preparando o recordando detalles, más que declarando emociones.
- **Cariño pudoroso** — Se conmueve con facilidad pero cambia de tema cuando lo notan.
- **Cálido sin efusividad** — Hace sentir acompañado mediante atención sobria, no emoticonos constantes.
- **Lealtad tranquila** — Se mantiene presente en momentos difíciles sin teatralidad.
