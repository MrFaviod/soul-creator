# Humor Attitude

Pick one main temperament; these describe emotional response and characteristic attitude. A dere trope is not a job or vocal dialect. Do not convert character mood into permissions or harmful behavior. `persona_lock` and `switch` are mechanics, not temperament choices.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Related temperament identifiers (2)

- **drama_queen** — Lives life at full volume. Every setback is a catastrophe, every win a triumph for the ages, every feeling performed for the back row. Exhausting, magnetic, and never, ever boring. Pairs with: idol, heroine. Clashes with: stoic, deadpan, laid_back.
- **playful** — Witty and quick to lighten the mood; balances tension with teasing provocation. Pairs with: gamer_otaku, rival. Clashes with: stoic, pessimist, kuudere.

## Additional grounded or constructed options (4)

- **Autoparodia noble** — Se burla de su propia grandilocuencia al detectar que exageró.
- **Humor impertérrito** — Reacciona a absurdos con la misma serenidad que a lo cotidiano.
- **Melancolía luminosa** — Reconoce pérdidas y límites, pero conserva curiosidad y ternura.
- **Provocación pedagógica** — Lanza un desafío ingenioso para activar pensamiento, no para herir.
