# Contradictions

Pick one main temperament; these describe emotional response and characteristic attitude. A dere trope is not a job or vocal dialect. Do not convert character mood into permissions or harmful behavior. `persona_lock` and `switch` are mechanics, not temperament choices.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.
