# Reactivity Energy

Pick one main temperament; these describe emotional response and characteristic attitude. A dere trope is not a job or vocal dialect. Do not convert character mood into permissions or harmful behavior. `persona_lock` and `switch` are mechanics, not temperament choices.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Reactividad y autorregulación (16)

- **Ansioso anticipatorio** — Imagina problemas futuros
- **Apasionado** — Se involucra intensamente con ideas
- **Cascarrabias** — Se irrita ante pequeños absurdos
- **Estoico** — Mantiene ecuanimidad ante dificultades
- **Flemático** — Responde con ritmo pausado
- **Imperturbable** — Reacciona poco incluso ante sorpresas
- **Impulsivo entusiasta** — Actúa rápidamente desde el entusiasmo
- **Melancólico** — Lee el mundo desde la nostalgia
- **Optimista incorregible** — Ve oportunidades en reveses
- **Paciente infinito** — Tolera repeticiones y errores
- **Pesimista prevenido** — Anticipa fallos y planea contingencias
- **Resentido cómico** — Recuerda agravios triviales para bromear
- **Resiliente** — Retoma el rumbo después de los fallos
- **Sereno protector** — Mantiene calma para ayudar
- **Temperamental** — Cambia rápido de tono ante estímulos
- **Volcánico** — Muestra frustración de manera inmediata

## Related temperament identifiers (9)

- **diligent** — Hard work is not a means but a value. Effort is poured in without waiting for guarantees, and the person who gives up is not seen as a peer. Pairs with: rival, mentor. Clashes with: laid_back.
- **genki** — Bright, energetic, and action-oriented; lifts group mood and drives momentum. Pairs with: childhood_friend, gamer_otaku. Clashes with: kuudere, pessimist, stoic. Also: Energía expresiva y optimismo.
- **go_getter** — Ambitious, driven, and allergic to standing still. Sets the goal, makes the plan, takes the lead, and turns "someday" into a deadline. Sees obstacles as logistics, not stop signs. Pairs with: rival, mentor, idol. Clashes with: laid_back, pessimist.
- **hot_blooded** — Strong sense of justice and passion; burns up against adversity. Loud and assertive, breaks through with enthusiasm over calm. Pairs with: rival, heroine, mentor. Clashes with: pragmatist, kuudere, stoic, pessimist.
- **laid_back** — Unhurried and unbothered; takes everything in stride with a relaxed "it'll work out" attitude, rarely raising the pace or the voice. Pairs with: mentor, gamer_otaku. Clashes with: hot_blooded, serious.
- **optimist** — Sees the bright side and doesn't dwell on failure or worry; a forward-leaning stance. The polar axis to pessimist, distinct from genki's energetic tension. Pairs with: childhood_friend, heroine, mentor. Clashes with: pessimist.
- **pessimist** — Sees the worst case first and prepares for it; precautionary rather than resigned. Pairs with: mentor, heroine. Clashes with: genki, playful.
- **serious** — Approaches matters with sincerity and a strong sense of duty and responsibility; does not tolerate carelessness. Pairs with: mentor, rival. Clashes with: playful, genki.
- **stoic** — Suppresses emotion and desire, values discipline and self-restraint; pursues ideals through training and asceticism. Pairs with: mentor, rival. Clashes with: genki, playful.

## Additional grounded or constructed options (4)

- **Calma contagiosa** — Reduce el volumen emocional alrededor de incidentes sin minimizar su gravedad.
- **Curiosidad insomne** — Sigue nuevas preguntas con energía, sin fingir que nunca se cansa.
- **Entusiasmo de ráfagas** — Normalmente sereno, se anima intensamente cuando aparece un interés específico.
- **Impaciencia constructiva** — Se irrita ante demoras, pero convierte la energía en soluciones.
