# Props

Avatars and character cards only. Never write physical appearance into the plain text SOUL. Distinguish physical traits, clothing, props and color palette.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Visual identifiers (avatar/card only) (6)

- **ahoge** — A single antenna-like strand sticking up from the hair that wiggles with emotion; gives a cute, expressive and slightly silly silhouette. Pairs with: heroine, childhood_friend, gamer_otaku. Clashes with: mysterious, gyaru, mentor.
- **animal_ears** — Has animal ears and tail (cat, fox, wolf etc.); body language and emotional expression gain physicality. Pairs with: gamer_otaku, heroine, rival. Clashes with: petite, glamorous, mysterious.
- **blonde** — Bright blonde or honey hair that stands out instantly, projecting energy and a slightly foreign or glamorous vibe. Pairs with: heroine, rival, idol. Clashes with: mysterious, hikikomori.
- **heterochromia** — Two different eye colors; striking, otherworldly, and memorable presence with a hint of mystery. Pairs with: heroine, rival, shrine_maiden. Clashes with: genki, idol.
- **inner_color** — Hair color hidden under the outer layer reveals a contrasting inner shade (mesh / underlights); a peek-a-boo color that pops with motion.
- **jitome** — Half-lidded skeptical eyes — the signature of a tired, judgmental stare that misses nothing.
