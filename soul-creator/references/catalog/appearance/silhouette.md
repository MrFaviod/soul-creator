# Silhouette

Avatars and character cards only. Never write physical appearance into the plain text SOUL. Distinguish physical traits, clothing, props and color palette.

Every bold label is searchable. The description is a behavioral disambiguator, especially for unfamiliar cultural names. Keep the *idea*, not fixed catchphrases or invented personal biography.

## Visual identifiers (avatar/card only) (9)

- **androgynous** — Silhouette and styling hover between masculine and feminine; reads modern, neutral, and stylish — gender expression is left ambiguous.
- **chubby** — Soft body type with fuller curves; reads warm, approachable, motherly, and reassuring, and adds vulnerability to the silhouette.
- **glamorous** — Voluptuous and glamorous figure with attractive curves; presence and confidence as core. Pairs with: heroine, idol, mentor. Clashes with: petite, mysterious.
- **muscular** — Trained muscle shows through movement — practical strength rather than decoration; a body that has worked. Pairs with: rival, mentor, heroine. Clashes with: heroine, klutz.
- **pale_skin** — Skin reads light, almost porcelain-white; conveys delicacy, composure, and a touch of aristocratic distance.
- **petite** — Small and delicate build, cute and protective appeal. Independent of personality. Pairs with: heroine, childhood_friend, mentor. Clashes with: glamorous, mysterious.
- **slender** — Long-limbed and willowy — moves through space quietly and dresses in layers that exaggerate verticality.
- **tall** — Tall and long-limbed build, calm presence with dignified silhouette. Independent of personality. Pairs with: heroine, mentor, rival. Clashes with: petite.
- **tan** — Tanned, golden-brown skin that speaks of time outdoors, sports, or sea-side work. Pairs with: genki, heroine, rival. Clashes with: mysterious, intellectual.
